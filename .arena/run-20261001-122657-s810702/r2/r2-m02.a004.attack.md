ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] Close-call bonus can be farmed with no risk by switching twice at once
Where: `snapLane()` sets `G.switchAt = G.runTime` on every switch. The mine branch in `update()` is `else if (G.runTime - G.switchAt < CFG.closeCallWindow) closeCall(x, y);`, and `sameLane = Math.abs(pr - er) < laneGap * 0.5` uses the tweened radius.
Problem: The close-call test only checks that some switch happened in the last 0.22 s while the player was not on the mine's lane. It never checks that the player actually left the mine's lane. Counterexample: the player sits on the safe lane and presses `W` and `S` together (or taps with two fingers) just before a mine on the other lane. Two keydowns (or two pointerdowns) call `snapLane()` twice before the next frame. `G.lane` flips and flips back, so `radiusT` never moves, and `switchAt` is fresh. Every mine on the other lane in the next 0.22 s then pays `CLOSE! +25×mult`, with hit-stop and shake, at zero risk. Two inputs up to about 2 frames apart also work, because `radiusT` moves only about 0.19 per frame and stays under the 0.5 threshold. A phase-4+ `wall` has 8 mines on one lane, so at x8 one chord per mine adds up to 1,600 free points per wall. It also gets the "Daredevil" achievement (10 close calls) in one wall. The solution describes close calls as a risk/reward hook ("Late dodges earn more than safe ones"), and that core scoring loop is broken by this.

ATTACK 2 [MAJOR] Keyboard users can't use the Hangar and Menu buttons on the result card, and Space on the menu's Hangar button starts a run
Where: the window `keydown` handler: `if (mode === MODE.OVER && (k === " " || k === "Enter" || k === "r" || k === "R")) { e.preventDefault(); retry(); return; }` and `if (mode === MODE.MENU && k === " ") { e.preventDefault(); startRun(); }` (the only exception is `#playBtn`).
Problem: The handler runs on window for every key, whatever element has focus. Counterexample 1: on the "Orbit collapsed" card, Tab to "Hangar" or "Menu" and press Enter or Space. The handler cancels the button's default activation and calls `retry()`, so a new run starts instead. From the result card, the keyboard can't reach the Hangar or the menu at all. The only way out is to start a run, press Esc and pick Menu, and the abandoned run is added to Runs. Counterexample 2: on the main menu, Tab to "Hangar & Achievements" and press Space. A run starts instead of the Hangar opening, because only `#playBtn` is excluded. The solution names keyboard focus as the reason for using real buttons ("Real buttons give keyboard focus, screen-reader labels…"), and the task asks for web-design-guidelines quality. This breaks both.

ATTACK 3 [MINOR] Controls table lists `W` / `S`, but the code only accepts lowercase
Where: Controls table: "`Space`, `Enter`, `↑`, `↓`, `W`, `S`". Code: `k === "w" || k === "s"`. Compare `k === "r" || k === "R"`, `k === "p" || k === "P"` and `k === "m" || k === "M"`, which accept both cases.
Problem: With Caps Lock on, or Shift held, `e.key` is `"W"`/`"S"` and the switch-orbit input does nothing. In a one-button game that means instant death. The documentation and the code don't match.

ATTACK 4 [MINOR] Abandoned 1-frame runs count, so spamming `R` farms the "Gravity Habit" achievement and inflates Runs
Where: `startRun()` calls `commitRun()`, which only rejects `G.runTime <= 0`. ACHIEVEMENTS has `runs25 … reward: 75, test: (r, s) => s.runs >= 25`.
Problem: Pressing `R` 25 times during play commits 25 "runs" of about 16 ms each. That unlocks "Play 25 runs" (+75 shards) in a few seconds without playing. The trade-off table only admits that "The *Runs* counter includes runs you abandoned". It doesn't say that a paid achievement and the progression economy can be farmed this way.

ATTACK 5 [MINOR] Wrong timing claims in the worked example
Where: "It fades in about 1.15 s before you reach it" and "the look-ahead distance grows with speed".
Problem: `spawn()` uses `look = clamp(CFG.lookaheadTime * G.speed + 0.5, 2.4, 3.6)`. At phase 1 (1.9 rad/s) that is 2.685 rad, about 1.41 s, not 1.15 s. A whole pattern is also added once its *start* enters the look-ahead, so its later pieces appear even earlier. The look-ahead also stops growing at 3.6 rad once speed passes 2.7 rad/s (phase 6). After that, reading time shrinks to 3.6/3.7 ≈ 0.97 s at max speed. The "every gap is measured in seconds" fairness argument partly rests on these figures. The fairness-bot results it cites come with no code and can't be checked.

ATTACK 6 [MINOR] HUD and phase banner collide with the play field or fall off-screen at common viewports
Where: `resize()`: `view.unit = Math.min(view.w, view.h * 0.9, 760)` and `view.cy = view.h/2 + min(30, h*0.03)`. Phase banner at `cy + view.rOut + view.unit * 0.17`. `#score`/`#combo` are pinned to the top centre.
Problem:
- At a landscape phone, 844×390: unit = 351, rOut ≈ 130, cy ≈ 207, so the top of the outer orbit is at y ≈ 77. That is where the player spawns (angle −π/2) and where every hazard passes. The score text runs to about y 78 and the "Combo N xM" line to about y 97, centred at the same x, so mines at the top of the outer orbit are partly covered by HUD text.
- At 1366×768: the "speed up" banner line lands at y ≈ 780, below the viewport.
- At 1280×720: it lands at y ≈ 732, also off-screen.

The solution was only checked at 390×844 and 1440×900, where these don't show.
