ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. Correct: persist() wrote blind and nothing listened for `storage`, so a second tab could overwrite a higher best. Fixed: every write first merges storage in (records take the larger value, cores are combined, running totals become the stored total plus this tab's increase, with the checkpoint's extra run subtracted so nothing is counted twice), and a `storage` listener merges the other tab's writes live. The "New best" badge also checks against the merged best.
Checked in headless Chromium with two tabs: the attacker's exact scenario now ends at best 500, 5 runs, 55 orbs. The reverse interleaving (checkpoint, then the other tab dies, then this tab dies) gives 2 runs, 12 orbs, best 80.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. Correct: orb glows were keyed on the continuously eased hue, so the cache grew without limit. Fixed: orb glows use the hue rounded to 6-degree steps (60 colours at most), and the cache itself is capped at 128 sprites, dropping the oldest first (8 MB at most). Measured 63 sprites after 80 lap colour changes.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. Correct: an offset of up to 0.5 s into a 1 s buffer cut short noises longer than 0.5 s, including the 0.7 s death sound. Fixed: the buffer is now 2 s, and the offset is limited to 2 s minus the sound's length minus 0.05 s, so every noise has its full length of samples.
