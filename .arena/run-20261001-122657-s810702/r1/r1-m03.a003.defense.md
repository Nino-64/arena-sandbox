ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. The anti-misclick guard was right, but taking 0.9 s + 0.45 s before a tap could restart was too slow on touch, where there is no R key. Now the panic tap is ignored only for 0.35 s. After that, a tap skips the death animation and goes straight to the results, and a tap 0.3 s later restarts. That cuts the touch restart to about 0.65 s, and the score is still always shown. Checked in headless Chromium: dying to playing in about 0.75 s with taps only.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. The menu-only guard that dropped arrows and WASD went against the controls table. I removed it, so every listed action key now starts a run from the menu. A focused trail button still keeps its own arrow and Enter behaviour, because the onButton check runs first. Verified: ArrowUp on the menu now starts play.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. The shared 32-voice counter let a burst of sound effects silently drop beat notes. Voices are now counted per bus, 24 for SFX and 16 for music, in tone() and noise() through track(node, gain, bus). The two buses can no longer starve each other.
