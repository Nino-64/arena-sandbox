ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. The flaw is real, but the example's numbers are wrong. At 30 fps on a 375 px phone the step is about 16 px, which is less than the 22 px hit diameter, so it can't tunnel there. It does tunnel at the 20 fps clamp: a drifting spike gives about 27 px against 22.8 px. It also tunnels on large screens at 30 fps: 2560×1440 gives about 68 px against 53 px.
Fix: `frame()` now sub-steps `update()` so that no step is longer than the comet's radius. Computed worst case at 20 fps and top speed: 5.5 px against an 11.4 px radius at 375×667, and 12.9 px against 26.6 px at 3840×2160. Tunnelling is now impossible.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. The gap check only ran at spawn time, and a drifting spike could close it later. Fix: in `update()`, a drifting spike reverses before it gets closer than `hazardGap()` to any spike on the other ring. I ran a simulation of 20,000 random 8 s pairs. The minimum cross-ring separation was 0.450 rad, which equals the gap, so the fairness rule now holds for the spike's whole life.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. The arithmetic is right: 0.95 + 0.45 ≈ 1.4 s, so the "about 1 s" claim was wrong. Fix: the new `retry()` accepts a tap, Space, ↑, W, Enter or R from 0.3 s after death, even while the death animation is still playing. It saves the run through `endRun()` first. The results screen now appears at 0.8 s and also uses the 0.3 s guard. The documentation now states these real numbers.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. The HUD wrote BEST only once, at the start of the run. Fix: once your score passes the record, `updateHud()` shows the live score as BEST, turns the readout gold and pops "NEW BEST!" once. `startGame()` resets all of this.
