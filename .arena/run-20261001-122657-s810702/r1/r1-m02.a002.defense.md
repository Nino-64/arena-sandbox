ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. The attack is correct. `visibility` stays `visible` for 0.28 s after `.show` is removed, so the fading `<section>` catches taps on top of the canvas. Fix: `.screen` now has `pointer-events:none` and `.screen.show` has `pointer-events:auto`. Pointer-events changes at once while the opacity still fades, so the first tap after Resume, Retry or Play reaches the canvas and calls `flip()`.
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. The attack is correct. R mid-run and Menu from pause skipped `finishRun()`, the only code that writes the save. Fix: the save logic now lives in `commitRun()`. A new `bankRun()` calls it on R during play, on R while paused, and on Menu from pause. It writes best, shards, XP, the level bonus and achievements, then shows a toast: "New best · N banked" or "Run banked · +N shards saved".
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. The attack is correct. On an 844×390 phone the summary needed a scroll, and every scroll gesture began with a `pointerdown` that restarted the run. Two fixes:
- `#over` now restarts on `click`. A touch that turns into a scroll fires `pointercancel` and no click.
- A `@media (max-height:540px)` compact layout keeps the card at about 330 px. With `place-items:start center` and `.card{margin-block:auto}`, the top of a card can no longer be clipped when it overflows.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. The attack is correct. `.toast.out`, `.skin.nope`, the screen fades and the XP-bar transition all got past the narrow selector. Fixes:
- Reduced motion now applies `*,*::before,*::after{animation:none!important;transition:none!important}`.
- The death and unlock flash drops from 0.35 to 0.1 alpha under reduced motion.
- Hit-stop and slow-motion are deliberately kept, because they change time, not on-screen movement. The doc now says exactly this.
ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. The attack is correct. `deadT` does not advance during the 0.1 s hit-stop, so the summary appeared after 0.95 s. Fix: the threshold is now `deadT > 0.75`, so the summary really appears 0.85 s after the hit. The doc states the split as 0.1 s hit-stop plus 0.75 s slow-motion.
