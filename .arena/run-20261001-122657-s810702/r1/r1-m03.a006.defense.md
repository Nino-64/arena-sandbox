ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. The pointerdown handler accepted any button and any pointer, so a second finger or a right-click could flip the ring and undo a dodge. Fixed with `if (!e.isPrimary || e.button !== 0) return;`. New tests: T26 (a right-click and a non-primary pointer leave the lane unchanged) and T27 (a primary click still flips).
ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. Speed and pattern density both stopped changing at about score 160, so "keeps getting tighter" was false. Speed now eases from 3.2 towards a ceiling of 3.8 rad/s (`+0.6*(1-exp(-(score-150)/400))`), and zipper frequency rises until score 760. Fairness was checked again: T28 shows speed rising (3.20, 3.48, 3.73 at scores 150, 400 and 1000). T30 has a reflex bot survive 60 s at score 3000 in 10 out of 10 runs. The prose now describes the curve exactly.
ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. A 2.6 rad look-ahead is less than π, so the claim was wrong. Look-ahead raised to 3.3 rad, which is more than half an orbit and at least 0.87 s at top speed. T29 checks this after the opening frame. The prose now names the one exception: the opening arc, about 1 s away at the starting speed.
ATTACK 4: CONCEDE. The reduced-motion setting was read once at load. The game now keeps the MediaQueryList and reads `MQ.matches` each time it is used, so a mid-session OS change applies on the next hit. T31 switches `reducedMotion` with `emulateMedia` without a reload and checks for the reduced shake.
