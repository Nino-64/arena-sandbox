ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. All four defects were real: N0 produced nothing countable, no UI measured the 95 %, the only rule jumped N0→N2 and never defined N3, and a per-agent "50 in 14 days" gate starves monthly actions.
Rule 1 is rewritten. Counters are per action type. A "décision tranchée" is an N1 card, or an N0 intent judged in a new « Revue » tab (scored automatically when you did the task yourself). Every step has a numeric gate: N0→N1 = 7 clean days; N1→N2 = at least 50 decisions, at most 2 corrected, last 20 clean; N1→N3 = the same on drafts, then 5→15 sends/day while bounces stay under 3 % and no complaint arrives.
Added: a control sample with automatic demotion after promotion, re-checking after a model change or budget fallback, and for rare actions a 12-month shadow replay (random sample of 50) or a permanent N1. The manifest now declares ceilings instead of the contradictory `defaut: N2`.

ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. No source was named for a swap ETF's index composition, and the issuer's holdings file often lists only the substitute basket.
Fixed: country, sector and currency weights now come from the issuer's monthly factsheet, which describes the index. The company view borrows the holdings file of a physical ETF on the exact same index (not an IMI or ESG variant), labelled « approximation ». It is hidden if its country weights drift more than 2 points from the factsheet. Full index constituent lists are licensed, so they are not scraped.

ATTACK 3: CONCEDE. HT + TVA = TTC only detects an inconsistency: 983,33 + 196,67 = 1 180,00 is just as consistent, so the check cannot say which field is wrong.
Fixed in both the capability and the scenario. The check now only flags; the exact bank debit decides (118,00 €, and no 1 180,00 € debit). With no deciding debit, the invoice stays « lecture incertaine » and is never matched on a guess. A zero-VAT micro-entrepreneur invoice (art. 293 B CGI) is no longer treated as an anomaly.

ARGENTIER's capabilities list `mail.brouillon.demande`, which the Agent Mail executes (Gmail `drafts.create`, Graph or IMAP `APPEND`); ARGENTIER has no right to send. A wrong follow-up date was fixed too: she is back Thursday the 15th, so the follow-up goes on Monday 19/10, not Friday 16/10.

ATTACK 5: CONCEDE. The push trigger rested on an undefined status, and the badges (« officiel ») did not even match it.
VIGIE's capabilities now state a tunable rule: « 1 source »; « corroboré » = 2 independent sources; « confirmé » = 3 independent sources, or a primary source for what the actor itself decides or announces. A party's claims about its adversary cap at « corroboré ».
The interruption threshold is explicit: « confirmé » AND an impact of at least 1 % of the portfolio, computed by ARGENTIER. The scenario, badges and rumour row follow the rule, and the brief's missing 4th event is now accounted for (it came from the poisoned blog and waits for corroboration).
