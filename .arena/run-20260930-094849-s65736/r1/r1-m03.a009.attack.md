ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] Product characteristics invented in the body and in subjects 5 and 6, and not all flagged
Where: Body: "La Reniala R1, une machine à café conçue pour les espaces de bureau". Subject 5: "Café de spécialité au bureau : La Reniala R1". Subject 6: "De la part de Colette : café de spécialité au bureau". Point 4 of section 4.
Problem: Constraint 1 bans any "caractéristique ... absent du contexte". The context says only "une machine dédiée aux espaces de bureau" made by a specialty-coffee company. Subjects 5 and 6 go further than "machine à café": they say the R1 brings *specialty* coffee to the office, which is a product claim the context never makes. Point 4 flags only "machine à café" and gives a fallback for the body only. Subjects 5 and 6 are not flagged and have no fallback. Counterexample: the user reads point 4, learns the machine is not a coffee brewer (or not a specialty one), fixes the body as told, then sends subject 5. The subject line still makes the unverified claim. That also breaks constraint 4 ("signaler explicitement toute incertitude").

ATTACK 2 [MAJOR] Invented figure "20 minutes" in the body and a subject line, plus a sample date left in the "ready to send" text
Where: Body: "un court échange de 20 minutes ... [créneau 1, ex. : jeudi 8 octobre à 10 h]". Subject 3: "Votre offre café chez Have a Good Day : 20 minutes d'échange ?"
Problem: Constraint 1 lists "chiffre" among the things not to invent. The context says only "un court échange". "20 minutes" is a number found nowhere in it, and it appears in two places the recipient sees: the body and subject 3. The note at the end of point 7 comes after the fact, and subject 3 is not covered by it. Section 1 is also titled "prêt à envoyer", yet its placeholder contains a real-looking date ("jeudi 8 octobre à 10 h"). If the user fills in only the obvious part of the bracket, that invented slot reaches Illan.

ATTACK 3 [MINOR] False grammar claim used to justify a change
Where: Section 3: "« Évoluant dans le secteur…, nous avons » : participe mal rattaché (grammaticalement, c'est « Colette » ou « l'attention » qui évolue)"
Problem: This is factually wrong. In "Évoluant dans le secteur du café de spécialité, nous avons développé une machine...", the participle attaches to "nous", the subject of the main clause. That is the correct construction. It is not dangling, and it cannot grammatically refer to "Colette" or "l'attention", which belong to the previous sentence. The answer presents an error as fact to the user, and constraint 4 forbids presenting unverified claims as fact.

ATTACK 4 [MINOR] Contradictory Colette rule: consent required for one phrasing, ignored for equally strong ones
Where: "Variante ... à utiliser seulement si Colette a accepté d'être citée comme recommandation". Point 3: "N'utilisez la variante « de la part de Colette » et l'objet n° 6 que si elle est d'accord". Subjects 1 and 2: "Colette (South World Wines) : ..." / "Suite à ma rencontre avec Colette ...". Section 3 table: "ce que le contexte ne dit pas".
Problem: The answer makes Colette's consent a condition for "de la part de" and for subject 6. Yet the default body and the recommended subject 1 put her name in the first line and the subject line as the reason for writing, with no condition. If her consent matters, subject 1 needs it just as much. If it does not, the variant rule is pointless. The user cannot tell which subjects are safe without guessing. The table also says the context does not present Colette as a recommendation, but the context's own heading reads "Recommandation : Colette". So the answer presents its own reading as a fact about the context.

Where: "Avant de vous la présenter plus en détail, j'aimerais surtout mieux connaître votre offre café actuelle..." followed by "Si vous souhaitez vous faire une idée d'ici là, voici la présentation de La Reniala R1 : [lien]"
Problem: The message first says the product presentation will come later, after the call. Two paragraphs on, it gives the presentation anyway. The reader gets mixed signals about what the call is for. "Avant de vous la présenter" also turns the call into a pitch, while the stated goal is to learn about Illan's current coffee offer and explore synergies. That works against the one thing the message is meant to get.
