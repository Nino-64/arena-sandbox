ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] The recommended default subject and a body sentence present an unverified referral as fact, which the solution itself says it cannot claim
Where: Subject 1 "De la part de Colette : l'offre café chez Have a Good Day" (tagged "Le plus sûr", "Mon conseil : utilisez le 1"). In the body: "C'est elle qui m'a donné l'idée de vous écrire."
Problem: The solution's own "À vérifier" point 1 says "Rien n'indique qu'elle vous a *recommandé* de le contacter". It limits subject 2 ("m'a conseillé de vous écrire") to the case where Colette really suggested the contact. But "De la part de Colette" makes the same claim: it tells Illan that Colette sent this message. That is exactly the implication of the base message's "de la part de Colette", which a sound rewrite should remove. Yet this subject is the unconditional default. The body sentence "c'est elle qui m'a donné l'idée" also credits Colette with an intention the context does not support (context: she "lui a parlé de la mission d'Illan"). This breaks constraint 4: "Signaler explicitement toute incertitude plutôt que de présenter une affirmation non vérifiée comme un fait". Counterexample: Illan asks Colette about it, she says she never suggested the email, and the sender's credibility is damaged in the first line he read.

Where: Subject 3 "Le café chez Have a Good Day : 15 minutes d'échange ?" compared with the body "Auriez-vous [15 à 20 minutes] dans les deux prochaines semaines".
Problem: The body treats the duration as a variable the sender should adjust ("La durée est entre crochets : ajustez-la").
