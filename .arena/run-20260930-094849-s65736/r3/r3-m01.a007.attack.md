ATTACK 1 [MINOR] The "Rien n'a été ajouté" audit is wrong: the body adds text the audit never mentions, including a commitment
Where: Section 3, point 7: "Rien n'a été ajouté. [...] Deux reformulations seulement [...] Aucun chiffre, client, prix, caractéristique ou promesse." Compare with the body: "Cela a retenu mon attention, d'où ce message.", "Vous pouvez la parcourir à votre rythme.", "Si un autre moment vous convient mieux, je m'adapterai volontiers."
Problem: The body contains more than "deux reformulations". "que j'ai eu l'occasion de croiser" becomes "que j'ai rencontrée". "Je souhaiterais échanger" becomes "j'aimerais surtout". "Auriez-vous des disponibilités" becomes "Seriez-vous disponible". The three sentences quoted above are new and are not in the base message or the context. "je m'adapterai volontiers" also commits Nino to Illan's schedule, and constraint 1 lists "promesse" among the things not to add. The problem is less the wording than the audit itself: section 3 exists so the user can trust that nothing was added, and on this point it is false. That runs against constraint 4, "Signaler explicitement toute incertitude plutôt que de présenter une affirmation non vérifiée comme un fait".

ATTACK 2 [MINOR] "parcourir à votre rythme" hints at the file's format, while section 3 says it does not
Where: Body: "Vous pouvez la parcourir à votre rythme." Section 3, point 1: "sans rien affirmer sur le contenu ni sur le format du fichier."
Problem: The context says: "Le contenu de ce document n'est pas accessible : ne rien affirmer sur ce qu'il contient". A drive.google.com/file/d/.../view link can point to a video, a PDF or a slide deck. "Parcourir" means leafing through a document. If the file is a video, "parcourir à votre rythme" is the wrong word, and section 3's claim that nothing is said about the format is false. A neutral sentence such as "Vous trouverez sa présentation ici" with no follow-up sentence avoids the problem. The solution adds the sentence and then says it implies nothing.

ATTACK 3 [MINOR] The recommended subject depends on Illan recognising "Colette", but there is no placeholder to identify her
Where: Recommended subject "Colette m'a parlé de vous – un court échange sur le café ?". Body: "Colette, que j'ai rencontrée [...]". Section 4, "Relation entre Colette et Illan".
Problem: Section 2 chooses subject 1 because of the "hypothèse non vérifiée : qu'Illan connaisse Colette". Section 4 flags that the relationship is uncertain. But the solution never gives the user a way to make "Colette" recognisable: there is no "[Nom de famille]" or "[structure]" placeholder. Constraint 1 says to leave a bracketed placeholder "Là où une information manque". A first name on its own is exactly the missing information that decides whether the referral works. Counterexample: Illan knows two people called Colette, or met her only briefly. Subject 1 and the first sentence then read like a cold email that name-drops a stranger, and those are the elements the solution relies on most.
